Commercial salon construction services in Detroit: what you need to know

Detroit's beauty industry is growing fast. Independent stylists are opening their own studios, suite style salons are filling storefronts in Midtown and Downtown, and new barber and beauty brands are choosing neighbourhoods like Corktown, New Center, and Eastern Market for their next location.

November 28, 2026 · 6 min read · Salon Studios
Table of contents

    If you are planning one of these spaces, you already know the creative side. What often surprises owners is the construction side. A salon is not a simple retail fit out. It has plumbing, electrical, and ventilation needs that decide whether your daily operation runs smoothly or fights you every day.

    This guide explains what salon construction involves in Detroit, how to plan the work, and what to look for in a salon studio construction company in Detroit before you commit.

    Why Salon Construction Needs a Specialist Approach

    A salon looks calm and simple when it is finished. Behind the walls, it is a technical build with several systems working together.

    Plumbing Is the Decision You Cannot Easily Undo

    Every shampoo bowl needs hot and cold supply and a proper drain. Those lines are set in the floor and walls early in construction. If you move a station later, you may pay to open floors and redo pipework. That is why your layout should be settled before any rough in work begins.

    Electrical Demand Is Higher Than Most Tenants Expect

    Dryers, flat irons, colour processing lamps, lighting, and a reception computer all run during peak hours. A panel sized for a standard retail unit can trip repeatedly. A good commercial general contractor in Detroit reviews your station count and equipment before finalizing the electrical plan.

    Ventilation Protects Your Team and Your Clients

    Colour, bleach, and styling products release fumes. Proper ventilation keeps the air comfortable and helps meet workplace health expectations. Mechanical systems should be planned from the start, not added once complaints begin.

    Commercial Construction Services in Detroit for Salon Owners

    Platinum Construction supports salon and beauty studio owners with a full set of commercial construction services in Detroit. Here is how the main options work.

    New Salon Build Out

    This takes an empty commercial space and turns it into a working salon. It includes layout planning, plumbing and electrical rough in, ventilation, framing, finishes, fixtures, and final inspections. For owners moving into a new unit, a build out gives full control over the design.

    Salon Renovation and Remodeling

    Many owners already have a space that feels dated or cramped. Commercial remodeling contractors can add stations, upgrade the shampoo area, refresh finishes, and improve flow. A commercial renovation company should inspect existing systems before pricing, because older buildings may hide wiring or plumbing problems.

    Design Build

    With a commercial design build contractor in Detroit, one team handles both design and construction. This keeps decisions moving, reduces gaps between drawings and the real space, and gives you one group accountable for budget and schedule.

    Construction Management

    If you already have a designer, commercial construction management gives you a dedicated lead who coordinates trades, tracks costs, and keeps inspections on schedule.

    Key Elements to Plan Before Construction Starts

    A few early decisions save the most money later.

    Station Count and Layout

    Decide how many styling stations, shampoo bowls, colour areas, and treatment rooms you need. Think about how stylists move and how clients flow from reception to chair to checkout.

    Lighting and Atmosphere

    Good lighting matters in a salon. Stylists need accurate light for colour work, while clients want a warm, welcoming feel. Plan task lighting and ambient lighting together.

    Flooring and Finishes

    Choose surfaces that handle hair, chemicals, and constant foot traffic. Durable, easy to clean flooring reduces maintenance and keeps the space looking sharp.

    Storage and Back Bar

    Product storage, colour mixing areas, and laundry space often get forgotten. Planning them early keeps the front of the salon clean and efficient.

    Permits and Inspections

    Commercial salon projects in Detroit generally involve city permit and inspection steps for building, plumbing, and electrical work. Timelines vary with the scope, so build permit planning into your schedule from the start.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What does a salon construction company in Detroit do?

    It plans and builds salon spaces, including plumbing, electrical, ventilation, finishes, permits, and inspections.

    How long does salon construction take?

    Most projects take a few months from planning to opening, depending on size, scope, and permit timing.

    Why does salon plumbing need early planning?

    Shampoo bowl lines are set in the floor and walls, so moving them later is expensive.

    Do I need permits for a salon renovation in Detroit?

    In most cases yes. Plumbing, electrical, and structural work typically needs permits, and your contractor should plan for them early.

    What is the difference between design build and construction management?

    Design build combines design and construction in one contract. Construction management coordinates the build when you already have a designer.

    How can I avoid budget surprises in an older building?

    Ask for a site assessment, a written itemized estimate, and a contingency of ten to fifteen percent.
