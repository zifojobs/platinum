Custom Homes & Renovations

Commercial discipline, residential craft.

Luxury custom residences, major additions and high-end renovations, managed with the same rigour as our commercial projects.

Thirty years of commercial contracting changes how a house gets built. Fixed budgets, real schedules, professional trades and documented quality control are standard on our residential work, because they are standard on everything we build. From a full custom home to a major addition or a top-to-bottom renovation, one team carries the project from drawings to keys.

Scope of work

What this service includes.

Custom home construction

New residences built from your architect's plans or under design-build.

Major additions

Structural additions and second-storey expansions integrated seamlessly.

High-end renovations

Complete interior renovations, kitchens and baths finished to exacting detail.

Permits & approvals

Zoning, permits and inspections handled from application to sign-off.

Transparent budgeting

Commercial-grade cost reporting so you always know where the budget stands.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

What size of residential project do you take on?

Full custom homes, structural additions and substantial renovations. Our commercial project management is what we bring to the table, so the work suits projects where budget and schedule discipline matter.

Do you offer design-build for homes?

Yes. We can carry both design and construction under one agreement, or build from drawings your architect has already prepared.

Where do you build homes?

Primarily across the Greater Toronto Area and southern Ontario. Contact us to discuss your location.
