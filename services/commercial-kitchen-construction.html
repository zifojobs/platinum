Commercial Kitchens

Kitchens built to code and brand standard.

Restaurant and commercial kitchen construction: ventilation, plumbing, gas, refrigeration and equipment installation, health-code compliant. A kitchen is the most technical room in any restaurant. Hood and makeup air systems, grease management, floor drains, gas lines and refrigeration all have to pass inspection and then survive daily service. Platinum has built kitchens for national quick-service brands and independent restaurants alike, and we coordinate equipment vendors so installation day is not a surprise.

Scope of work

What this service includes.

Ventilation & hood systems

Exhaust hoods, makeup air and fire suppression installed and balanced to code.

Plumbing, gas & drainage

Floor drains, grease interceptors and gas lines routed for daily operations.

Equipment coordination

Vendor scheduling, rough-in verification and final equipment installation.

Health-code compliance

Finishes and layouts that pass public health and building inspections.

Front-of-house build-out

Dining rooms, counters and service lines finished to the brand standard.

Frequently asked questions.

Do you install kitchen equipment?

We coordinate the full equipment package: rough-ins are verified against the spec sheets, vendors are scheduled, and the line is installed, connected and tested before handover.

Will the kitchen pass health inspection?

Compliance is designed in from the start. Washable finishes, drainage, ventilation and food-safe layouts are built to the local public health requirements, not corrected afterwards.

Can you renovate a kitchen in an operating restaurant?

Yes. We phase the work, often overnight, so the restaurant can keep serving while the kitchen is upgraded.
