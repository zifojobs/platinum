Commercial & Industrial Developments

Ground-up developments, delivered to spec.

Large-scale plazas, industrial facilities and base-building construction with complete sitework. One accountable team from excavation to handover.

For more than 30 years, Platinum Construction has delivered ground-up commercial and industrial projects across Canada and the United States. We manage the full scope in house: site servicing, foundations, structure, envelope and interior finishes. Owners deal with a single partner from the first drawing to the final inspection, with clear budgets and a schedule that holds.

Scope of work

What this service includes.

Site preparation & servicing

Excavation, grading, utilities and municipal coordination to make the land build-ready.

Base-building construction

Structure, envelope and core systems built to specification and ready for tenants.

Plaza & multi-unit retail

New retail plazas and multi-unit developments, from shell to tenant handover.

Industrial facilities

Warehouses, automotive and light-industrial buildings engineered for daily operations.

Landlord & tenant coordination

We align base-building work with tenant fit-out schedules to avoid costly delays.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you build ground-up or only renovate?

Both. We deliver complete ground-up developments, including sitework, and we also take on major renovations and facade programs for existing commercial and industrial buildings.

Which regions do you cover?

We build across Canada through Platinum Construction Corporation and across the United States through Platinum Construction of America, Inc. Recent work spans Ontario, Alberta and Texas.

Can you work from our architect's plans?

Yes. We price and build from your issued-for-construction drawings under a lump sum contract, or we can take design responsibility as well under a design-build agreement.
