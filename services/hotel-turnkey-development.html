Hotel Turnkey Developments

Hospitality projects, ready for day one.

Full turnkey delivery: we design, build, furnish and hand over a hotel that is ready to welcome its first guest.

Hotel projects succeed when construction and operations are planned together. Platinum delivers the complete package: design-build construction, brand compliance, FF&E procurement and installation, and pre-opening coordination. The owner receives one contract, one schedule and a property that is operational from day one.

Frequently asked questions.

What does turnkey mean in practice?

You sign one agreement and receive a finished, furnished, operating-ready hotel. Design, construction, FF&E and commissioning are our responsibility, not a list of separate contracts to manage.

Do you renovate operating hotels?

Yes. We phase the work so the property can keep renting rooms, and we coordinate directly with management to protect the guest experience.

Can you work with hotel brands and franchises?

We build to the brand's published standards and manage their inspection process, from prototype drawings through final sign-off.
