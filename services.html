Our process

Contract options.

Three contract structures, three ways to engage — choose the one that fits your project's risk profile, budget control needs, and delivery timeline.

01

Lump Sum Contract

We provide fixed pricing based on your plans, providing full cost clarity for financing and forecasting.

02

Project Management Contract

We act as the Owner's representative, overseeing planning, coordination, scheduling, budgeting, and quality control while the Owner retains decision-making authority.

03

Design / Build Contract

We manage both design and construction under a single contract. It streamlines communication, reduces timelines, and improves cost control from concept to completion.
