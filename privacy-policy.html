Your information, and what we do with it.

Platinum Construction Corporation and Platinum Construction of America, Inc. respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

We collect personal information for one reason only: to respond to the enquiries you send us and to prepare the work you ask us to quote. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

The details.

01

Who we are.

02

What we collect.

Information you give us. When you submit the form on our Contact page, we receive your full name and email address, which are required, and your company, phone number, and address, which are optional, together with the description of your project that you write in the message field.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard technical data when a page is served: IP address, browser type, device type, the page requested, and the date and time. This data is used to operate the site securely and to diagnose faults.

We do not ask for, and this website does not process, payment information, financial details, health information, or government identifiers.

03

Why we collect it.

We use the information you send us to reply to your enquiry, to prepare and discuss an estimate, to carry out a project if you engage us, and to keep the business records that a general contractor is required to keep.

We rely on your consent, which you give by choosing to submit the form. You can withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us, subject to any records we are legally required to retain.

04

Who we share it with.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We share it only with the service providers that make this website work, and only to the extent each one needs:

Vercel Inc. hosts the website and processes the technical data described above.
Web3Forms delivers your contact form submission to our inbox.
Google Fonts serves the typefaces used across the site.
Google Maps displays the maps embedded on our Contact page.
Matterport and Digital Properties host the virtual tours embedded on our Virtual Tours page.

These providers operate servers outside Canada, including in the United States, so information handled through this website may be stored or processed abroad and may be subject to the laws of those countries.

We may also disclose personal information where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim.

05

Cookies and tracking.

This website does not set cookies of its own, and it does not run analytics or advertising tracking. We do not build profiles of visitors and we do not use your visit to target advertising.

One small item is stored by your browser during your visit: a single entry that records that you have already seen the short introduction on our home page, so that it is not shown to you again. It holds no personal information, it is not shared with anyone, and your browser discards it as soon as you close the tab.

Content embedded from third parties can set its own cookies once it loads, specifically the Google Maps frames on our Contact page and the virtual tour frames on our Virtual Tours page. Those cookies are governed by the privacy policies of Google and of the tour providers, not by this one. Your browser settings let you block or delete them.

If we ever introduce analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy before doing so.

06

How long we keep it.

We keep the information you send us for as long as it takes to answer you, and afterwards for as long as we need it to manage the relationship and to meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations. When it is no longer needed for any of those purposes, it is deleted or anonymized.

07

How we protect it.

The site is served over HTTPS, form submissions are transmitted encrypted, and access to the inbox that receives them is limited to the people who need it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information involved.

08

Your rights.

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to give you access to it, to correct it if it is inaccurate or incomplete, or to delete it where we are not required to keep it. You may also withdraw your consent to our use of it.

Write to info@platinumconstruction.com. We will respond within the time frame set by applicable law, and we may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

09

Children.

This website is intended for businesses and property owners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10

Changes to this policy.

We may update this policy as our practices or the law change. The date at the top of this page always shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Questions about this policy, or about the information we hold on you? Write to info@platinumconstruction.com or call 905-763-8119 in Canada, 917-793-0462 in the United States.
