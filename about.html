Our story

30+ years of building quality across North America.

Platinum Construction has successfully navigated every economic cycle, emerging stronger with each one.

What began as a regional contractor has evolved into a trusted construction partner for some of North America's most recognized brands.

Our growth has always been driven by a simple principle: deliver uncompromising quality on time, every time. That commitment continues to define every project we build today.

Philosophy

We are only as good as our clients.

This principle, voiced by our founder Gaetano Alaimo, still drives every decision we make. Partnering with great clients, and earning their trust through craftsmanship, transparency and delivery, is the key to our enduring success.

01 / Craftsmanship

Quality without compromise.

Every nail, every joint, every finish is built to stand the test of time. We answer only to our own standards.

02 / Transparency

No hidden surprises.

Clear scopes, open books, honest timelines. Our clients always know where they stand, at every stage.

03 / Delivery

On time. On budget. On brand.

Our network of trusted vendors and expert managers makes turnkey delivery our signature strength.
