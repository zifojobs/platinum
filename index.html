About the company

Trusted partner to North America's leading brands.

Platinum Construction Corporation & Platinum Construction of America Inc. have grown from local contractors into trusted leaders in commercial construction across North America, delivering projects for renowned brands including Chipotle, Starbucks, Panera Bread, McDonald's, Firehouse Subs, Dairy Queen, Tim Hortons, Wendy's, and many more.

Our founder's philosophy remains at the heart of everything we do: "We are only as good as our clients." Building lasting partnerships and earning trust through craftsmanship, transparency, and dependable delivery has been the foundation of our enduring success.
